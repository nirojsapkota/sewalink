# Poster Guide: How to Post and Manage a Task

*Knowledge Base Factsheet · For Posters · Version 1.0*

## Step 1 — Create a task

From your dashboard, tap "Post a Task." Add a clear title, category (e.g. Cleaning, Moving, Repairs, Tutoring, Delivery), description, budget, and location. You can mark a task as "On-site" if the tasker must physically be at a location to check in and complete the work.


## Step 2 — Set your budget

Budgets are entered in Nepali Rupees (NPR). You can post a fixed budget or leave it open for taskers to bid their own price. All amounts you see (task budget, bid amounts, final payment) already include or clearly show the platform commission, so there are no surprise fees at payment time.


## Step 3 — Review bids

Once published, your task is visible to nearby taskers who can submit bids with their price and a short note. Compare bidder ratings, completed task counts, and reviews before choosing. You can message a bidder directly through in-app chat before accepting.


## Step 4 — Assign & track

Accepting a bid moves the task to "Assigned," then "In Progress" once the tasker starts work. If the task is on-site, you'll see a live status update when the tasker checks in near the task location.


## Step 5 — Approve completion & pay

When the tasker marks the task complete (with a required completion photo for on-site tasks), you review and confirm. Funds you already placed in escrow are then released to the tasker automatically.


## Cancelling a task

You can cancel a task before it is assigned at no cost. Cancelling after assignment may be subject to platform policy and could require admin review, especially if a tasker has already started work.


## Tips for posters

- Write specific descriptions — clearer tasks get better bids faster.
- Check tasker ratings and past reviews before hiring.
- Use in-app messaging instead of sharing personal phone numbers early on.
- Attach photos of the job site or item when relevant.
- Leave an honest review after completion to help the community.
