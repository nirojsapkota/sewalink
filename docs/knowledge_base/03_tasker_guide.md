# Tasker Guide: Finding, Bidding, and Completing Tasks

*Knowledge Base Factsheet · For Taskers · Version 1.0*

## Getting started as a tasker

Switch your active role to "Tasker" from your profile. Complete your profile with a photo, skills, and categories you're interested in — this improves how often relevant tasks are shown to you.


## Finding open tasks

Browse the "Open Tasks" feed, filtered by category, distance, and budget. Tasks show whether they are on-site (require physical presence and location check-in) or remote.


## Submitting a bid

Tap a task and submit your price along with a short message explaining your experience or approach. You can revise a bid before it is accepted.


## Getting assigned

If a poster accepts your bid, the task moves to "Assigned." You'll get a real-time notification. From there, tap "Start Work" to move the task to "In Progress."


## On-site check-in (geofencing)

For on-site tasks, when geofencing is enabled by the platform, you must be within about 200 meters of the task's location to start work or mark it complete. Make sure location services are enabled in your browser.


## Marking a task complete

When finished, upload a completion photo (required for on-site tasks) and mark the task complete. The poster will review and confirm before your payment is released.


## Getting paid

Once the poster confirms completion, your earnings (task price minus the platform commission) are released from escrow into your SewaLink balance. You can then request a payout to your bank or eSewa wallet.


## Building your reputation

- Complete tasks on time and communicate proactively.
- Ask posters for reviews after every job.
- Keep your profile and skills up to date.
- Respond quickly to messages and bid invitations.
