# Payments, Escrow & Commission FAQ

*Knowledge Base Factsheet · For Posters & Taskers · Version 1.0*

## How does payment work?

When a poster accepts a bid, the agreed amount is collected upfront via eSewa (Nepal's digital wallet and payment gateway) and held in escrow by SewaLink. The tasker only receives payment after the work is verified as complete, protecting both sides from fraud or non-delivery.


## What is escrow?

Escrow means SewaLink holds the payment securely between poster and tasker until the task is confirmed complete. Neither party can access the funds directly during this period.


## What is the platform commission?

SewaLink deducts a commission percentage (configured centrally and shown transparently before you accept a bid or a job) from the task amount before crediting the tasker's balance. This funds platform operations, dispute support, and payment processing.


## How do I get paid as a tasker?

After a task reaches "Payment Completed," the net amount (task price minus commission) is added to your SewaLink balance. From there you can request a payout to your bank account or eSewa wallet through the Payouts section.


## How long do payouts take?

Payout requests are reviewed and processed by SewaLink's finance/admin team, typically within a few business days, and you'll get a notification once it's completed.


## What if I paid in cash?

For some tasks, payment may occur in cash on-site. In this case the platform still records the commission owed and reconciles it separately so accounting stays accurate.


## Common issues

- Payment not deducted: check your eSewa balance and that the transaction was confirmed — contact support if a charge appears on your bank statement without an in-app confirmation.
- Escrow not released after completion: the poster may not have confirmed yet, or a dispute may be open — check the task status page.
- Payout delayed: verify your payout bank/eSewa details are correct in your profile.
