# Safety, Geofencing & Location Check-In Policy

*Knowledge Base Factsheet · For Posters & Taskers · Version 1.0*

## Why geofencing exists

For on-site tasks, SewaLink can require the tasker to be physically present at the task location before they can start work or mark it complete. This protects posters from being charged for work that was never actually performed at the correct location.


## How it works

When geofencing is enabled platform-wide, the app checks the tasker's live GPS coordinates against the geocoded task address. If the tasker is within roughly 200 meters, they're allowed to check in and proceed; otherwise the app blocks the action and asks them to get closer.


## What if geofencing is off?

SewaLink admins can disable geofence checks platform-wide (e.g. for testing or for regions with unreliable GPS/location data). When disabled, on-site tasks can be started and completed without a location check.


## Enabling location access

Taskers should allow location permissions in their mobile browser when prompted. Without location access, on-site check-in and completion cannot be verified.


## General safety tips

- Meet or communicate only through the app until you've built trust.
- For high-value or in-home tasks, consider a video call before confirming.
- Report any suspicious behavior immediately through the Help/Support section.
- Never share your OTP, password, or payment PIN with anyone, including someone claiming to be SewaLink support.
