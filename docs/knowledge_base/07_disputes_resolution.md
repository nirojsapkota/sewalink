# Disputes & Resolution Process

*Knowledge Base Factsheet · For Posters & Taskers · Version 1.0*

## When to raise a dispute

Raise a dispute if a tasker doesn't show up, the work delivered doesn't match what was agreed, there's disagreement over payment amount, or a poster refuses to confirm clearly completed work.


## How to raise a dispute

Open the task and tap "Raise Dispute." You'll be asked to describe the issue and can attach evidence — photos, messages, or other documentation supporting your side.


## What happens next

Raising a dispute moves the task to "Dispute" status and immediately pauses any pending escrow release. A SewaLink admin is assigned to review the case.


## Evidence review

Admins review submitted evidence (completion photos, chat history, check-in/location logs, and any disputed evidence files) before deciding how to resolve the case fairly.


## Possible outcomes

- Full release to tasker — if evidence supports that work was completed as agreed.
- Full refund to poster — if evidence shows the task was not completed or was materially misrepresented.
- Partial settlement — split between both parties when evidence is mixed.
- Task reassignment — in rare cases, a task may be reopened for a new tasker.


## How long does resolution take?

Most disputes are reviewed within a few business days. You'll be notified in-app as soon as an admin makes a decision.


## Tips to avoid disputes

- Agree on scope and price clearly before assignment.
- Use in-app messaging to document expectations.
- Take before/after photos for on-site work.
- Confirm completion promptly once you're satisfied.
