# Reviews & Ratings Guide

*Knowledge Base Factsheet · For Posters & Taskers · Version 1.0*

## Why reviews matter

Reviews build trust across the marketplace. A strong review history helps taskers win more bids and helps posters attract better responses from serious taskers.


## When can I leave a review?

Reviews unlock once a task reaches "Payment Completed." Both the poster and the tasker can rate each other on a 5-star scale and leave a short written comment.


## What should a good review include?

- Whether the work matched the agreed description.
- Punctuality and communication quality.
- Whether you'd hire/work with this person again.
- Specific, constructive feedback rather than generic comments.


## Can reviews be edited or removed?

Reviews are generally permanent once submitted to preserve trust in the rating system. If a review is abusive, false, or violates SewaLink's community guidelines, either party can report it for admin review and possible removal.


## How ratings affect visibility

Taskers with consistently higher ratings and more completed tasks tend to appear more prominently in bid lists and search results, so maintaining quality work pays off over time.
