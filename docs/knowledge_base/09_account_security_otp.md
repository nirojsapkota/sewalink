# Account, Login & Security FAQ

*Knowledge Base Factsheet · For All Users · Version 1.0*

## How do I sign up?

SewaLink uses your Nepali mobile phone number as your login identifier instead of email. Enter your number, verify it with a one-time code (OTP), and set up your profile.


## What is OTP login?

Every login and sensitive action (like changing payout details) can require a One-Time Password sent via SMS, and optionally email, for an extra layer of two-factor security.


## Accepted phone number format

SewaLink accepts standard Nepali mobile numbers starting with 96, 97, or 98 (10 digits total, e.g. 98XXXXXXXX).


## Switching between poster and tasker

Your account has a single profile but an "active role" you can switch between Poster and Tasker at any time from your account menu — no need for separate accounts.


## What if I lose access to my phone number?

Contact SewaLink support with proof of identity so an admin can help you regain access or migrate your account to a new number.


## Keeping your account safe

- Never share your OTP code with anyone — SewaLink staff will never ask for it.
- Use a strong, unique password in addition to OTP.
- Log out of shared or public devices after use.
- Report suspicious messages claiming to be from SewaLink support.
