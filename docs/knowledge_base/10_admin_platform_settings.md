# Admin & Platform Settings Guide

*Knowledge Base Factsheet · For Admins · Version 1.0*

## Who is an admin?

Admins are SewaLink staff with elevated permissions to moderate content, resolve disputes, manage payouts, and configure platform-wide behavior. Admin access is granted through the internal role system and is default-deny — every action must be explicitly authorized.


## Platform Settings overview

A single, platform-wide settings record controls shared behavior for all users, including:


## Key configurable settings

- Commission rate — the percentage SewaLink deducts from each completed task.
- Geofence check-in toggle — whether on-site tasks require live GPS proximity to start or complete work.
- Category management — creating/editing task categories with English and Nepali names.
- Featured/priority settings for categories or promoted taskers, where applicable.


## Dispute resolution responsibilities

Admins review disputed tasks, examine submitted evidence (photos, messages, check-in logs), and decide whether escrow funds are released to the tasker, refunded to the poster, or split.


## Payout management

Admins review and approve tasker payout requests, verifying bank/eSewa details before funds are released outside the platform.


## Accounting & reconciliation

A separate accounting workflow lets admins import eSewa settlement CSV files and reconcile them against internal payment records to ensure every transaction is accounted for, and to generate ledger reports.


## Content moderation

Admins can review flagged tasks, messages, or reviews that violate community guidelines and take action such as removal, warnings, or account suspension.


## Best practices for admins

- Document the reasoning for every dispute resolution decision.
- Change commission rates conservatively and communicate updates clearly.
- Regularly reconcile settlement reports against the internal ledger.
- Review flagged content promptly to keep the marketplace trustworthy.
